Philosophy

"Flavors found only here, in this moment."
An intimate hideaway uniting once-in-a-lifetime culinary encounters.

Located in Ichibancho, Sendai, Sushi & Washoku Enn offers an original style of Japanese cuisine rooted in traditional Edomae sushi.

We carefully pair both aged and fresh seafood—meticulously prepared to draw out peak umami—with shari crafted from locally grown Miyagi Sasanishiki rice, balanced in acidity and richness to complement every piece.

From seasonal small plates, appetizers, and grilled delicacies to the climax of ten signature nigiri pieces—each course is orchestrated in rhythm and depth for a deeply fulfilling dining experience.

Designed in serene Japanese tranquility for unhurried conversation, we dedicate our honed skills and heartfelt hospitality exclusively to just four parties each evening.

Craftsmanship

The Luxury of Only Four Parties a Day.

At Enn, we welcome no more than four parties each evening.
Discerning the ideal aging and preparation for every fish,
our head chef personally steps up to your table to craft each piece at its peak moment.
This is our aesthetic—sharpening uncompromising technique to deliver truly attentive hospitality.

Traditional Edomae Preparation

Carefully selected seasonal fish undergo meticulous Edomae preparation to unlock their purest natural umami.
Savor the harmony of aged delicacies, red-vinegar seasoned shari, and the finest bounty of the four seasons.

Shari Rice Miyagi Sasanishiki (Red Vinegar Blend)

Crafted Right Before Your Eyes

Stepping beyond the traditional counter, our chef comes directly to your table
to hand-press each piece of sushi right before you.
Experiencing the artisan's graceful movements and fresh aroma up close
creates an intimate performance reserved solely for four parties a night.
